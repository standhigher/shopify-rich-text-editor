# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a professional, useful, and respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying precise about technical risks.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Avoid sharing private merchant data, credentials, tokens, or internal business details.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private data or credentials.
- Repeated off-topic comments or spam.
- Personal attacks instead of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or community.
